# Base-case analysis summary

- Scenario: `base_high_risk`
- Perspective: public healthcare payer
- Incremental cost: JPY 54,945 per exposed contact
- Incremental QALYs: 0.003267 per exposed contact
- QALY gain attributed to modeled acute mortality avoidance: 0.000459 (14.1% of total QALY gain)
- ICER: JPY 16,817,443 per QALY
- Incremental net monetary benefit at JPY 5,000,000/QALY: JPY -38,609
- Cost-effective at the reference threshold: False
- Symptomatic cases prevented: 75.37 per 1,000 treated contacts
- Hospitalizations prevented: 0.592 per 1,000 treated contacts
- Deaths prevented: 0.0710 per 1,000 treated contacts
- Surviving symptomatic cases contributing to the post-acute profile prevented: 75.30 per 1,000 treated contacts
- Number needed to treat to prevent one symptomatic case: 13.27
- Maximum total PEP intervention cost consistent with zero INMB: JPY 19,121
- Maximum drug acquisition cost, holding other PEP costs fixed: JPY 11,021

This is a manuscript-development base case, not a reimbursement conclusion. Severe-outcome effects are indirectly mediated through prevented symptomatic COVID-19 because the randomized trial observed no COVID-19-related hospitalization or death. Remaining QALY loss is derived from an age-82, 54%-male proxy using the 2025 Japanese life table and Japanese EQ-5D-5L norms. The base case attributes all 28-day all-cause admissions in the outpatient claims source to COVID-19; 50% and 0% attribution scenarios quantify this structural uncertainty.
